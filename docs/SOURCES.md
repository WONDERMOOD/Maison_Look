# Research & sources

This is a Korean-language educational fashion dictionary. Descriptions are independently paraphrased; not product listings or manufacturer guarantees. Descriptions distinguish garment construction, hem length, opacity, and fabric weave.

- Fashion History Timeline at FIT — Fashion History Dictionary: https://fashionhistory.fitnyc.edu/dictionary/
- Marks & Spencer — Your Guide to Types of Tights and Stockings: https://www.marksandspencer.com/content/types-of-tights-and-stockings
- Silver Legs — Hosiery Glossary A–Z: https://www.silverlegs.co.uk/pages/glossary
- Journelle — Hosiery Guide: https://www.journelle.com/pages/hosiery-guide
- Hosieree — Hosiery Glossary: https://www.hosieree.com/glossary.html
- Fashion History Timeline at FIT — 1948 Dior Evening Ensemble (satin sheen as a historical example): https://fashionhistory.fitnyc.edu/1948-dior-evening-ensemble/

**Important distinctions:** denier measures linear density of yarn, not directly transparency; actual opacity depends on structure, colour, stretch and finish. Stockings (thigh length) may require garters, while hold-ups self-support. Tights/pantyhose commonly cover from waist to toe; leggings commonly stop at the ankle without covering the foot. Satin is a weave rather than a fibre type.
